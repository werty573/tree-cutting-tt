Good questions, straight answers.

Do I need permission to cut a tree on my land?

Usually not on private residential land, but some trees and sites need approval from the Forestry Division or the regional corporation. We'll tell you at the site visit if yours does.

What happens to the wood?

Your choice. We can cut logs to firewood length and leave the chippings as mulch, or haul everything away. It's priced either way upfront.

The tree is touching T&TEC lines. Can you help?

Branches on live lines must be handled with the utility. We help you make the request and do all the work around it once the line is safe.

How fast can you come out?

Quotes from photos often come back the same day. Planned jobs are normally booked within the week, and storm calls jump the queue.
